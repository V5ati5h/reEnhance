Quick Start Guide

From Blocks to Live Ads in 4 Simple Steps

You do not need any coding skills or complex software. Just follow this easy workflow:

1

Add the Extension to Your Project

Download dev.v5ati5h.reenhance.aix and import it into MIT App Inventor or Kodular (under Palette > Extension > Import extension). Drag the reEnhance component onto your screen.

2

Snap Your Ad Blocks

Always call LoadPayload once inside Screen1.Initialize first — it activates the real ad engine (or a live Companion demo if you're just testing). Then place one ad block per network/format wherever you want it to appear:

when Screen1.Initialize do
  call reEnhance1.LoadPayload
when reEnhance1.PayloadReady (version) do
  call reEnhance1.AdMobBanner ( layout: HorizontalArrangement1, placement: "main_banner" )
when ButtonWatchVideo.Click do
  call reEnhance1.AdMobRewarded ( placement: "extra_lives" )
when reEnhance1.RewardEarned (placement) do
  call Lives.Change ( amount: 1 )

Block names follow a simple pattern: <Network><Format> — e.g. AdMobBanner, AppLovinInterstitial, UnityRewarded. See the full Blocks Guide for every network and format.

3

Export Your App

In App Inventor, click Build > Android App (.apk) to download your app file to your phone or computer.

4

Open reEnhance Manager & Tap Enhance

Open the reEnhance Manager app on your Android phone, select your exported app, and tap Build Signed APK. Once ready, tap Install. That's it — your real ads are now working!

2. Ad Network Blocks

Every ad block follows the same pattern: <Network><Format>. Banner/LargeBanner/MREC/Native blocks take two inputs — layout (the Arrangement to dock the ad into) and placement (any text label you choose, used to tell ads apart in the event blocks). Interstitial/Rewarded/RewardedInterstitial/ AppOpen/Instream/Offerwall/Survey blocks take only placement and show fullscreen. A few networks name their App Open block slightly differently (noted below) because that's what the real block is called — no App Inventor block name is ever shortened to "ShowX" the way older docs for this project implied.

4. Choosing Which Networks Are Active

The blocks above are always available in the editor, but which networks you actually want to use is a choice you make in the reEnhance Manager app, not in your App Inventor blocks. Before you enhance your app, open the Manager, turn on the networks you have accounts with (and want to prioritize), and turn off any you don't — the Manager app walks you through this with a simple on/off list per network. Your blocks don't change; the Manager just makes sure only the networks you picked are ready to serve ads when your app runs. If you ever call a block for a network you turned off, that one simply reports AdFailed for that placement. 

// What a realistic rewarded-ad flow looks like end to end:
when Screen1.Initialize do
  call reEnhance1.LoadPayload

when reEnhance1.PayloadReady (version) do
  call reEnhance1.AdMobBanner (layout: BannerArrangement, placement: "home_banner")

when ButtonWatchAd.Click do
  call reEnhance1.AdMobRewarded (placement: "extra_lives")

when reEnhance1.RewardedCompleted (format, placement, rewardType, rewardAmount) do
  set Lives.Text to (Lives.Text + rewardAmount)

when reEnhance1.AdFailed (format, placement, message) do
  call Notifier1.ShowAlert (notice: "Ad unavailable: " + message)
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to get started smoothly.

Do I need any programming experience or code knowledge?
Not at all! If you can drag blocks in MIT App Inventor or Kodular, you can use reEnhance. You simply drag the extension, snap the ad blocks where you want them, and let the reEnhance Manager app prepare your app with one tap.
Will ads show up when testing with the MIT AI Companion?
The AI Companion is only designed for quick visual layout testing. Real ads require your actual app package. Simply build your app (.apk) in App Inventor and enhance it once with the reEnhance Manager app on your phone to see real ads live!
Why did my phone say "App not installed" or "Package conflict"?
Android checks the digital signature of every app. If you previously installed your app from App Inventor, Android will only let you update it if the signature matches. To fix this permanently: export your keystore from App Inventor (Projects > Export Keystore), import it once into reEnhance Manager under Signing Keys, and every update will install smoothly over your existing app without having to delete it first!
Can I test ads safely without risking my AdMob account?
Yes! In the reEnhance Manager app, simply switch on Test Mode. It automatically uses official Google sample ad units so you can test all your banners, rewarded videos, and interstitials safely without violating any ad policies.
Which ad networks can I use?
reEnhance exposes dedicated blocks for 26 ad networks: Google AdMob, Google Ad Manager, AppLovin MAX, Meta Audience Network, InMobi, Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Mintegral, Pangle, Start.io, Tapjoy, Unity Ads, AdColony, Leadbolt, Appnext, Chartboost, Fyber / Digital Turbine, MobFox, Ayet Studios, CPAlead, Admantum, AdGate Media, RapidoReach, TheoremReach, Wootric, Pollfish, and BitLabs — see the full Blocks Guide below for exactly which ad formats each one supports.
